Our Story

UPS Protection Sdn Bhd was established in 2001 with a vision to provide world-class electrical testing, commissioning, and maintenance services across Malaysia. Over the past two decades, we have grown to become a trusted name in the industry.

Our expertise spans across high-voltage and low-voltage electrical systems up to 275KV, serving industrial facilities, power plants, and infrastructure projects throughout Malaysia.
